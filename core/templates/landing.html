FAQ

Questions people ask before sending

Does this send emails automatically?

Yes. After you select recipients and launch a campaign, sending runs in the background.

Can I use Gmail SMTP?

Yes. Configure Gmail SMTP with an app password from the settings page.

Are certificates generated automatically?

Yes. MailForge can generate personalized certificate PDFs from your uploaded template.

Is my data stored securely?

The app is self-hosted, so your data stays in your own deployment and database.

Can I self-host?

Yes. The project is built as a Django application and can run on your own server.
